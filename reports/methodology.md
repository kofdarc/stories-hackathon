# Methodology

The [causal decision analysis](causal_decisions.md) distinguishes plausible mechanisms from identified effects. Price gates compare two assumed response paths: loss of the target item only versus loss of the full containing basket. With unchanged mix, uplift U, current target-item GP P and containing-basket GP B, break-even losses are U/(P+U) and U/(B+U). Per-product basket exposures overlap and cannot be summed. Attachment gates subtract separately assumed operating expense, displaced contribution and representative lost-basket GP from assumed incremental target-unit contribution. No queue delay, elasticity, quality response or customer lifetime value is estimated from unavailable fields.

Additional decision studies are documented in [deep_insights.md](deep_insights.md): category-label randomization preserving basket line counts within branch/month/order-type/daypart, BH correction across all category pairs, chronological descriptive replication, duplicate sensitivity, exact two-factor growth attribution and a seven-day-block GP/basket bootstrap. These diagnostics do not identify causal cross-sell response. The new interface follows the sidebar scope; the complete research brief is explicitly labeled as full-file evidence.

Source → validated lines → baskets and summaries → matched products → financial scenarios → reports. Run `python scripts/run_analysis.py`; no dashboard dependency is needed for business computations.

Financial amounts are supplied USD, never overwritten. Validation uses 0.00500001 USD to distinguish half-cent rounding from material exceptions; reports disclose exceptions. Summed source amounts are formatted to cents only for presentation. No duplicate deletion occurs. Metadata consistency includes missing customer identifiers.

Margins are sum(GP)/sum(revenue). AOV is revenue/distinct baskets. Category-filtered revenue and profit describe selected items; the dashboard's basket page restores complete baskets containing those selected items. Product/category basket counts overlap. Weekdays are ordered Monday–Sunday. Identified-customer results exclude anonymous baskets and cannot represent all customers.

Matched cost difference sums Garden quantity × (Garden cost − identical-product Harbour mean cost). Product-branch cost cardinality is audited; means do not establish procurement prices if costs vary. Common-mix comparison uses chain-wide quantity weights. The exact Shapley bridge attributes the Harbour–Garden difference in GP/unit to mix, list price, list-value-weighted discount and cost by averaging all 24 possible replacement orders; interactions are assigned once. A separate source-rounding residual reconciles the analytic formula to supplied recorded GP exactly. It is a descriptive arithmetic decomposition.

Scenarios preserve Garden product mix and discounts, apply cost recovery × proportional coverage, scale realized revenue by price adjustment, then reduce revenue and variable costs proportionally by assumed demand loss. Net benefit is change in recorded GP less one-time expense. Coverage is proportional across the product portfolio, not a count-based product selection. Break-even volume loss = (pre-loss scenario GP − baseline GP − expense)/pre-loss scenario GP. No forecast or confidence interval is attached to deterministic identities.

Basket pairs use presence rather than line repetitions. Training is Jan–Apr; validation is May–Jun; minimum training count is 30. Support = pair baskets/all baskets, confidence = pair baskets/A baskets, lift = pair count × baskets/(A count × B count). All eligible pairs are exported, including negative results. Rankings remain exploratory. Bundle scenarios target A-only baskets with one incremental B unit and a 10% list-price rebate on new and existing A+B baskets; use 2%, 5%, 10% assumed adoption and quantity-weighted historical B contribution. Cannibalization, operating costs and repeat behavior are unknown. Discounting all existing pairs creates leakage; break-even adoption explicitly offsets it. Negative results are retained.

Existing discount evidence adds product/context controls, date-cluster intervals, no-overlap reporting and fixed-basket label-shuffle diagnostics. Read `analysis/README.md` and its full report. Those estimates are descriptive, not treatment effects. No ML model is needed for fixed-cost reconciliation or deterministic decision thresholds.
