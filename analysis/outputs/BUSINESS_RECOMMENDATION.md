# Stories discount decision — 17 October 2026

**Hold expansion. Test 0%, 10% and 15% offers concurrently at Harbour before scaling.** January–June data contain 33,887 orders. Against no-discount baskets, 10% baskets contain 15.2% more units and $0.17 more recorded gross profit; 15% baskets contain 12.0% more units and $0.37 more GP. Those associations overstate what we know about incremental value: shuffling 10% item-line labels while keeping baskets fixed reproduces a +0.249-unit gap, versus the observed +0.238. Larger baskets are more likely to contain any discounted line. Product/time-matched 10% lines show essentially no additional units per line. Detailed context matching leaves a GP difference of +0.154 (95% interval -0.018 to +0.326), covering 49.4% of 10% baskets.

**The 15% story changes with the baseline.** Every 15% line is cold coffee or dessert at Harbour during 12–19 March or 12–19 May. Its GP/basket is $5.65, below Harbour’s $5.97 no-discount baseline. All eligible lines in those windows are discounted, so no same-date eligible untreated comparison exists. Against contemporaneous changes at the other branches, total Harbour GP/day differences are −$6.85 in March (95% interval −$75.28 to +$61.58) and −$25.88 in May (−$88.90 to +$37.14). These estimates are inconclusive about causality and do not justify a broad rollout.

**Estimated value is conditional.** Recorded list-price concessions total $2,178.09; this is not recoverable savings. At the observed reference product margins, a blanket 10% offer requires 20.5% extra unit volume and a 15% eligible-item offer requires 29.3% before program costs, assuming unchanged mix and costs. For 1,000 baseline units and an assumed 30% causal lift, estimated contribution after a $100 program cost is +$166 for 10% and −$79 for 15%. These are illustrative scenarios using different reference mixes, not a head-to-head treatment estimate or a forecast. Do not multiply observed GP/basket gaps into claimed savings.

**Next action:** randomize offers before purchase, include nonbuyers, and measure contribution per assigned visitor after product, incremental labor and program costs. Log eligibility, assignment and redemption; monitor daily GP, substitution and repeat visits. Pre-register budget, power and a minimum worthwhile profit gain. Current evidence covers only three branches, two short 15% windows, 36.5% anonymous orders and 132 unresolved repeated rows. Branch demand, targeting and serial dependence remain unresolved. Historical evidence does not establish October performance.
