# Proposed validation, never an experiment already performed

1. Finance audits the top five matched-cost dollar contributors. Retrieve supplier invoices, cost-master formulas, recipes, portion controls, freight allocations and inventory movements for the same products/months at Garden and Harbour. Record whether each difference is avoidable cash expense or allocation. Reject procurement intervention if the gap is accounting-only.
2. Where savings are verified, pilot eligible product procurement changes against matched unchanged products, using a pre-period and stable recipe/quality specifications. Track invoice cost per conforming unit, total contribution after incremental labor, stockouts, waste, preparation time and quality complaints. Finance sets spending cap E and a minimum worthwhile gain before launch. Stop on quality failures or service deterioration. Duration must cover supplier ordering cycles and weekday variation; use day-level variance and serial dependence to calculate adequate precision before setting a fixed duration.
3. Randomize 0%, 10%, 15% promotion eligibility before purchase, stratified by branch/daypart. Include zero-purchase visitors and retain assignment regardless of redemption. The primary metric is contribution per assigned visitor after product and incremental program costs. Secondary metrics: conversion, units, cannibalization, complaints and repeat visits. For cross-selling, start with a no-discount suggestion versus control before paying rebates on existing pairs.
4. Specify minimum worthwhile contribution delta, alpha and power before enrollment. An initial independent two-arm approximation is n/arm ≈ 2 × (1.96 + 0.84)^2 × variance/delta^2; estimate variance using visitor-level pilot data including nonbuyers, then adjust for repeated customers, clustered assignment and three-arm multiplicity. Existing purchase-only data cannot supply that variance. Use intention-to-treat, customer/day cluster uncertainty as appropriate, and fixed stopping rules. Do not peek at daily significance or extrapolate an inconclusive result.
